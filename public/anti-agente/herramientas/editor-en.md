# Anti-Editor

Edit your videos with AI without leaving Anti-Agente. Give it a raw video, pick your template or tell your bot how you want it, and get it back edited: no pauses or repeats, with captions and vertical.

**Everything is free and runs on your computer.** Your Mac's Whisper does the transcript (the audio never leaves it) and your bot, with your own subscription, decides what to cut. No credits, no extra payments.

## How it works
1. **Drop your files** in Projects (or click to choose them), or give them to your bot in the chat. Whatever you drop together goes into one project: takes, music, images, PDFs or anything you want to use. They're used where they are: your originals are never touched.
2. **Tell it what each thing is.** Click a file to see it big and leave a comment (“the good take”, “show it when I talk about the price”). Write your instructions (if you want a different format from the original, say so: reels, shorts, YouTube or square) and pick a template if you want.
3. **Click “Edit”.** Your bot reads everything, asks you in the chat whatever isn't clear and decides what to cut (repeats, mistakes, filler). Pauses are removed automatically.
4. **It's edited** with your template's captions, colour and transitions, in the same format as your video (or the one you asked for). Meanwhile you see where it's at: transcribing, editing 2 of 5, checking. With Anti-Agente's video engine it runs in the background and you can close the screen; without it, on the screen in real time.
5. **Changes:** you see what was cut and can put it back with one click, or ask your bot for something else. Each change saves a new version without deleting the previous one.
6. **It checks itself:** when it's done, each video is checked (size, sound, volume, black screens, long silences and, if you asked for a network, text covered by its buttons). The volume is fixed automatically; anything else is flagged in Result.
7. **Result:** the edited video has its own tab. Stop it wherever you want to change something, note what at that exact second and, once you have them all, send them to your bot together. “Show in folder” opens the folder where it is.

## Resources
Everything you provide for a video goes into its project's resources folder: the video, other videos, images, PDFs, a script or any file. You can add them when you create it or later, by dropping them on the project or giving them to your bot in the chat. Your bot takes everything into account when editing and can put your images and videos on top while you talk.

## Clips, takes and music
- **Clips:** from a long video, your bot pulls out the parts that stand on their own, each with its hook, and tells you why each one works. They're edited as separate videos.
- **Several takes:** if you recorded in parts, they're joined in order and your bot keeps the best of what you repeated.
- **Music:** yours. It ducks when you talk and fades out at the end.

## Templates
Your saved style: captions (words at a time, height, size, colour, uppercase), zoom on the beats, transition between cuts (cut, fade or flash), colour (brightness, contrast, saturation and temperature) and voice clean-up. Click one to start a video with it; to change something, ask your bot. A template is only the style: each video keeps the original's format unless you ask your bot for another.

**Reviewers:** before editing, your bot goes over the cut as 1 to 3 people would see it: someone scrolling, someone short on time, someone new to the topic, a sceptic, someone who already knows, someone who was looking for it, someone watching with the sound off or someone about to buy. Each template comes with its own and you can ask for them to be changed. If your bot knows who your audience is, it takes them into account too, first. It applies what makes the video better, always after what you asked for.

## Ready-made formats
It comes with one template per format, which you can change or delete: captioned reel, clips from a long video, versions with different hooks, before and after, animated top list, tier ranking, whiteboard diagram, animated video with just your voice, illustrated reel, 15-second ad and YouTube video (landscape, no captions and 3 thumbnails to choose from). Texts, icons, cards and diagrams come in right when you say them.

## More editing
- Any video, with or without voice: join clips by the second, transitions, slow motion, zoom, videos or images on top, animated texts, split screen.
- A cover with big text over the best frame.
- Cut out the person: change the background behind you, put text behind you or follow your face when turning a landscape video into portrait.
- **AI images:** if you have ChatGPT connected in Anti-Agente, your bot can create illustrations or backgrounds for your videos. They use your ChatGPT plan, not credits.

## Limits
With Anti-Agente's video engine there's no length or size limit. Without it, each edited video can be up to 4 minutes, it's edited with the screen open and it works best with MP4 or phone MOV files.

## What it touches in your accounts and computer
- **Nothing in your accounts:** it doesn't publish or schedule on any network.
- **Your original videos are never touched.** It reads them where they are and the edits are saved separately, in its data folder.
- It doesn't install anything. Only the first time you cut out a person, the app asks you to download a small model (about 26 MB).
