# Anti-Web

Change your website by talking to your bots. You see it inside the app, point at what you want to change and ask for it, in writing or by voice. Everything happens in a draft first: your website doesn't change until you approve it.

## How to use it
1. **Pick your website** in the menu at the top. If it isn't there, use «Add website…» and choose its folder.
2. **Browse** it: click the address at the top to see the list of pages, or «Back».
3. **Edit:** click what you want to change (a heading, a button, an image) and it shows above the chat box. Type the change and press **Enter** to note it down; you can stack several, also from other pages. To talk to your bot about something else, remove the tag with its X.
4. **«Make the changes»** hands them all to your bot at once. You can also ask in the chat, typed or by voice («do it», «change the header image»). It makes them in the draft and the preview updates by itself.
5. **Review** the changes at the bottom. Each one has its own «Undo». You can view it as on a computer or on a phone.
6. **Publish** sends the changes to your live website. You always get a card first, and you decide.
7. If you don't like something afterwards, **«Go back to the previous version»** undoes the last publish, also with a card.

## New pages
In the page list, **«+ New page»**: give it a name, it's created in the draft and you see it straight away. Then type in the chat what it should have and your bot builds it. On WordPress and Shopify it's shown approximately and created on your site when you publish. Not available on GoHighLevel.

## Analysing websites
Your bots know how to review websites, but only when you ask:
- **«Analyse my website»**: five reviewers look at it on mobile and desktop (speed, SEO, copy, someone in a hurry and someone who rarely uses the internet) and give you a short report, with what pays off most at the top.
- **«Make me one like this»** plus the address of a site you like: it builds a similar one in the draft, with your brand and your copy. The structure and ideas are copied, never the other site's text or images.

## What you need
One of these, not all:
- A custom-built website in a folder on your computer with git, or on GitHub.
- A Shopify store, a WordPress site (with or without WooCommerce) or a GoHighLevel account connected in the app.

## What it touches in your accounts
- **While you edit**, only the draft changes: a separate copy of your website (in Shopify, an unpublished theme called «Anti-Agente · Borrador»). Your live website isn't touched.
- **When you publish**, it only uploads the files or content shown on the card.
- **Go back to the previous version** only undoes the last publish made with Anti-Web.
- It doesn't touch keys, passwords or secret configuration files.
- If something can't be changed from here (for example, pages built with Elementor or GoHighLevel funnels), it tells you, and your bot leaves the change ready for you to make by hand.

## What has been tested
- **Tested end to end:** custom-built websites in a folder with git. Browsing, pointing, editing, undoing, discarding, publishing, going back, and the computer and phone views have been tested.
- **Not yet tested with a real account:** Shopify, WordPress, WooCommerce, GoHighLevel and GitHub. The code is there, but it may fail. Each version will tell you what has been tested.
