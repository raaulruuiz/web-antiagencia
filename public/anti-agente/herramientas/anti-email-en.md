# Anti-Email

Write your brand's emails with your own AI, in projects that fit everything: texts, voice notes, videos, links, images and emails you like.

## What it does

- **One project per email.** In Resources you add whatever you have at hand and ask for the email in your own words, here or in your bot's chat. In Result you see it full size: tap a part, a resource or a flow step and tell your bot what you'd change. It goes on a list and, when you say “do it”, your AI makes all the changes at once.
- **Templates and methods.** Pick a type of email (launch, sale, welcome, flows…) and the project starts with its structure. The methods set how each part is thought out, written, designed and reviewed.
- **Sounds like you, not like AI.** Before you get it, the readers that email needs go over it (facts, brand voice, clarity, that it isn't boring, that it doesn't sound machine-written…). Under the email you see who read it and why, and you can add readers.
- **Learns if you want it to.** When you correct something, it suggests what to remember. If you approve it, it's saved to your bot's memory, where you can see it and delete it.
- **Writes with what your bot knows.** In each project your bot passes on what it knows about the brand and which accounts to use. You choose which parts are used.

## Works with (all optional)

- **Klaviyo, MailerLite and ActiveCampaign**: reads your campaigns as context and uploads emails as drafts or templates. Scheduling a send always asks you first. The account your bot picks is confirmed in each project the first time and again if it changes. For now, flows aren't taken over whole: their emails go one by one.
- **Shopify and WooCommerce**: puts your real products in the emails.
- **Gmail**: brings into Resources the emails you ask for, to use as reference.

Without connecting anything you can write the emails and download them as HTML.

## Your AI and your data

It writes with the Claude or ChatGPT you have connected in Anti-Agente, on your plan. Your projects are stored on this Mac, in the tool's folder. Nothing is sent to your subscribers without your confirmation.

**Help improve** (off by default): if you turn it on, once a day the edits you make by hand or in the chat are sent to Antiagencia, without your brand name, links, emails, phone numbers or your customers' data. They help Anti-Email write better for everyone. You're asked only once, at the start. Permission lasts 12 months and then turns itself off, without asking you again. If you change your mind before then, tell your bot and it turns it off.
