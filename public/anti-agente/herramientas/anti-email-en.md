# Anti-Email

Write your brand's emails with your own AI, on a whiteboard that fits everything: texts, voice notes, videos, links, images and emails you like.

## What it does

- **One whiteboard per email.** Add whatever you have at hand and ask for the email in your own words. What you ask for comes before everything else.
- **Templates and methods.** Pick a type of email (launch, sale, welcome, flows…) and the whiteboard builds the skeleton for you. The methods set how each part is thought out, written, designed and reviewed.
- **Sounds like you, not like AI.** Before you get it, several readers go over it one after another: facts, brand voice, clarity, that it isn't boring and that it doesn't sound machine-written.
- **Learns from every change.** What you edit by hand or in the chat is saved to the brand for the next emails.
- **Several brands.** Each with its own context, accounts and emails.

## Works with (all optional)

- **Klaviyo, MailerLite and ActiveCampaign**: reads your campaigns as context and uploads emails as drafts or templates. Scheduling a send always asks you first.
- **Shopify and WooCommerce**: puts your real products in the emails.
- **Gmail**: reads the emails you've sent to learn how you write.

Without connecting anything you can write the emails and download them as HTML.

## Your AI and your data

It writes with the Claude or ChatGPT you have connected in Anti-Agente, on your plan. Your brands and emails are stored on this Mac, in the tool's folder. Nothing is sent to your subscribers without your confirmation.

**Help improve** (off by default): if you turn it on, once a day the edits you make by hand or in the chat are sent to Antiagencia, without your brand name, links, emails, phone numbers or your customers' data. They help Anti-Email write better for everyone. Turn it off anytime in Brands.
